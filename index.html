About Our Chapter

Mu Epsilon Delta at the University Of Illinois Urbana-Champaign is a community of students pursing healthcare who are passionate about serving others, growing together, and most importantly building lifelong friendships along the way.

Brotherhood

Mu Epsilon Delta cultivates a diverse and supportive brotherhood of students pursing careers in healthcare, united by a passion for service and leadership.

Service

Through meaningful volunteer opportunities, Mu Epsilon Delta empowers members to give back to their communities and make a lasting impact.

Scholarship

Mu Epsilon Delta is committed to supporting academic success by providing mentorship, resources, and opportunities that help memebers achieve their professional goals in healthcare.
